# Reviewed P2 Checkpoint

Date: 2026-10-22. **P2 remains incomplete. Native feasibility is blocked.
Semantic search is disabled.**

This checkpoint separates reviewed implementation from the next output-adherence
protocol. It is not release, runtime-execution or feature-enablement approval.

## Included Work

- Request-owned evidence verification, strict output/span validation, monotonic
  deadlines, cancellation, cleanup and publication guards with shared regressions.
- Versioned V2 calibration policy tooling, frozen synthetic datasets, rejected
  pre-collection bytes, independent judgments and retained failed selection seals.
- V3 rubric, independently reviewed synthetic packs and bounded feasibility harness.
- Isolated V4 serial-verification QA, declared pressure profile, freeze/authorization
  checks, single-attempt ledger, strict classifiers and private frozen-APK staging.
- Audited archival-test migration retaining frozen tests and stale-source rejection.
- Review reports, failed-result summaries and the separate output-adherence brief.

No indexing, vector persistence, semantic-search UI or grounded-answer integration
is enabled. The V2 held-out corpus remains unused. V4 stopped after the first
invalid result; its seven remaining requests are not authorized to resume.

## Validation And Evidence

The reviewed wrapper checkpoint passed 89 host tests with no failures/skips.
The latest independent review also replayed the original installation race and
confirmed frozen-byte installation, temporary-copy cleanup, preserved evidence
hashes and rejection of old execution freezes against changed code. See
[wrapper details](P2_V4_WRAPPER_HARDENING.md).

Earlier Kotlin/build/device results remain tied to their recorded snapshots;
committing does not rerun or expand those results. The raw failed output and
strict validator remain unchanged. The blocked S24 outcome is retained in
[the pressure-profile report](P2_V4_PRESSURE_RESULTS.md).

Source, tests, synthetic fixtures and documentation are versioned. Model weights,
APKs, secrets, personal data and raw `benchmarks/results/` archives stay out of Git.
Small campaign manifests retain their existing local references and hashes; they
are not portable copies of raw device evidence. No local archive is deleted,
rewritten or replaced by this checkpoint.

The next change is a separately reviewed, versioned output-adherence protocol.
Implementation, execution freeze and S24 authorization remain separate gates.
