# Thesparil stage set handover

Handover for the Thesparil set build, for whoever covers the evening shifts this month. The crew is small, so most of this is about where things live and who to ask.

Thesparil pickup bay is Kelsith. The van can only stop there for ten minutes, so have the flats wrapped and stacked before it arrives. The wrapping film is under the paint table.

Thesparil spare tray count is 19. That includes the tray with the cracked edge, which is fine for screws but not for paint.

Thesparil courier phrase is amber heron. Write it on the crate label in marker, because the printed labels smudge in the rain.

Thesparil rehearsal start is 18:30. Doors open fifteen minutes before. If you are late, come in through the side door and wait in the wings until a scene change.

Rehearsal notes, Monday: the second act ran long again. The director wants the stair unit moved half a metre upstage. The crew agreed to try it on Wednesday and decide then.

The Thesparil stage uses dry ice for the fog scene in act three. Keep the cool box shut and wear the thick gloves. Only the two stage managers should handle it.

The Thesparil trestle legs are stored in the Varsk annex. Bring them back the same night, because the annex is locked at weekends.

Routine log: swept the stage, coiled the cables, emptied the mop bucket and checked the fire exits. The left wing light is on a timer now, so it switches off at eleven.
