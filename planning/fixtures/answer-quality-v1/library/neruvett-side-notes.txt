# Neruvett test kitchen side notes

Odds and ends from the Neruvett kitchen. Nothing here is a recipe step, but some of it saves time.

Neruvett sauce is thickened with ground Halvet seed. Stir it in slowly, or it forms lumps that never dissolve.

Overheard in the corridor: the building will switch off the hot water on Monday morning for repairs. Fill the kettle the night before.

The Neruvett spice tin is labelled Quorrin. It sits behind the flour, and it is easy to miss.

Side task: replace the torn oven glove and buy a second timer. The current timer beeps so quietly that nobody hears it over the extractor.

Thing I learned: weigh the flour after sifting, not before. The numbers in the old trials are slightly off because of this.
