# Neruvett test kitchen handover

Handover for the Neruvett recipe trials, for the weekend cover. The kitchen is small and shared, so tidy as you go and label everything with the date.

Neruvett pickup bay is Ardune. The supplier leaves the boxes on the left pallet; check the seals before you sign. Damaged boxes go back the same day.

Neruvett spare tray count is 33. The deep trays are on the bottom rack and the shallow ones on the top. Do not mix them in the dishwasher.

Neruvett courier phrase is velvet thistle. Say it at the hatch when the samples leave, and write it in the logbook with the time.

The Neruvett mixer has a standard UK mains plug, so use the adaptor in the drawer by the sink. Do not use the extension lead with the scorch mark.

Kitchen log, Saturday: cleaned the hob, defrosted the small fridge, threw out the old yeast. The scales were off by a few grams, so I reset them with the test weight.

The Neruvett tasting panel was led by Lusko Pervane this week. Scores are in the grey binder, one sheet per recipe.

Mireth Pallowin helps in the Neruvett kitchen on Tuesdays and Fridays. Leave her a note if the trial plan changes.

Neruvett oven temperature is 172 degrees.
