# Fendrovil dispatch

Dispatch notes for the Fendrovil parcels. Keep the sheet in the tray by the door so the runner can find it early.

The Fendrovil paint mark is Henvask. A copy of this line will go beneath the latch before closing.

The Fendrovil sleeve count is 29. I counted twice after putting the opened packet alongside the sealed ones.

Routine log: taped up the split boxes, swept the floor by the door, and refilled the label printer.

The Fendrovil delivery day is 2044-02-19. Please leave the envelope on the bench if nobody is at the door.

The Fendrovil collection time is 09:20. The Fendrovil tape supplier is Kemmoth.

The Fendrovil runner is Rellav Peshurin. The return bag has spare labels tucked inside its folded lip.

Meeting notes, Wednesday: the Fendrovil parcels for the north side will go out together from now on. Rellav prefers one long round to two short ones.

Side task: ask whether the Fendrovil sorting table can move nearer the window. The light by the door is poor in the afternoon.

Thing I learned: weigh the Fendrovil parcels after taping, not before. The tape adds enough to push a few into the next band.

Routine log, Friday: the Fendrovil returns shelf is half empty. Two parcels came back with the wrong street name, and they are waiting in the red crate for a new label.

Overheard at the counter: the Fendrovil depot will close early on the last Friday of the month, so the late round has to leave before noon that day.
