# Zevrikan weaving workshop side notes

Small things from the Zevrikan workshop that are worth keeping.

The Zevrikan warp threads were sorted by Ysolde Kravet on Monday. She grouped them by weight, lightest on the left.

Overheard at the fair planning meeting: the hall has no hooks this year, so we need our own display stands.

Zevrikan rugs are rinsed in the Pellow trough after dyeing. Leave them to drip on the rack before carrying them anywhere.

Side task: ask the guild whether they still lend out the big frame for the summer show.

Thing I learned: wind bobbins firmly but not tight. A tight bobbin pulls the edges in.
