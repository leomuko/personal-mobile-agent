# Thesparil stage set side notes

Bits and pieces from the Thesparil build. These are not instructions, just things worth remembering.

Thesparil scenery flats get a fresh coat of paint at the Ennolt shed. The shed has better ventilation than the theatre, and the drying racks are taller.

The Thesparil curtain hooks were counted by Avenna Thrisk after the last show. A few were bent, and she set them aside in a jar.

Overheard during the break: someone has been borrowing the good scissors and not putting them back. There is now a string tied to them.

Side task: find out whether the venue still lends out the tall ladder, or whether we need to hire one for the lighting focus.

Thing I learned: paint the edges of the flats last, because hands always end up on the edges when they are moved.

The Thesparil fog scene needs the dry ice delivered on the morning of the show, not the day before.
