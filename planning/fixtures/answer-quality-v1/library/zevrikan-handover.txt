# Zevrikan weaving workshop handover

Handover for the Zevrikan workshop, for the new evening volunteers. The looms are old and patient, but the people who use them are not, so please book a slot first.

Zevrikan pickup bay is Yarrowek. The yarn delivery arrives there on the first Monday of the month. Carry the bales two at a time, because they are heavier than they look.

Zevrikan spare tray count is 44. The trays hold bobbins, and each one has a coloured sticker for its loom.

Zevrikan courier phrase is quiet marten. Give it to the driver when finished rugs leave for the shop, and record the rug numbers in the logbook.

Zevrikan thread count is 312.

Zevrikan loom room is Drusk. The key hangs on the hook inside the office door.

Workshop log, Thursday: oiled the treadles, rewound four bobbins, swept up the lint under loom two. The radiator in the corner is stuck on high again, so I opened the window a little.

The Zevrikan loom frame is made of copper, so polish it with the soft cloth only. Abrasive pads leave marks that never come out.

The Zevrikan shuttle case is kept in drawer 72 of the tall cabinet. Put the shuttles back point first.
