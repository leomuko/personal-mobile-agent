# Velquorin instrument rig side notes

Loose notes from the Velquorin bench that did not fit anywhere else. Some of these are reminders, and some are things I learned the slow way.

The Velquorin shell gets its leak trial in the Oskel vat. A thin film of oil on the seals is enough. Wipe the vat rim afterwards, or the next person gets oily sleeves.

The Oskel vat is filled with fresh water each morning before the Velquorin trials start. Drain it on Fridays.

At Velquorin, the cradle weighs 41 grams after the liner is removed. Measure before attaching the feet. The feet add a little, but nobody has written down how much.

The Velquorin battery packs were charged by Pell Ostravin on Wednesday. They last the whole demo if the screen brightness stays low.

Overheard at lunch: the canteen is changing suppliers again, and the soup will be worse for a while. Also, the car park barrier is being repainted next week.

Side task: sort the drawer of loose screws by size. Half of them are from a different project and can go back to the store room.

Thing I learned: never stack the Velquorin rig cases more than two high. The bottom lid bows and the latch stops lining up.
