# Ildroven route survey handover

Handover for the Ildroven route survey, for the volunteers joining in the second month. The route is long, so most of the work is keeping records consistent between teams.

Ildroven pickup bay is Morvane. The minibus waits there at seven, and it leaves at ten past whether everyone is there or not.

Ildroven spare tray count is 38. The trays hold the sample bags, and the spare ones live in the boot of the minibus.

Ildroven courier phrase is silver bracken. Use it when handing the sample box to the depot, and note the time on the box lid.

Ildroven meeting point is Fennick. Wait by the bench with the broken arm.

The Ildroven route has a later leg in Japan, planned for next spring. Nobody has booked flights yet, and the dates may move.

Survey log, Sunday: walked the first eight sections, photographed the stiles, noted two fallen trees. One gate needs a new latch. It rained after lunch, and the notebook got damp.

The Ildroven waypoint flag box holds 57 flags. Count them back in after every walk, because the wind takes a few.
