# Ildroven route survey side notes

Things from the Ildroven walks that do not belong in the formal survey.

The Ildroven ford is only crossed after the river gauge drops below 34 centimetres. Above that, use the long way round by the mill.

Ildroven walkers refill bottles at the Tollen spring. The spout is behind the big holly, and the water is cold even in summer.

Overheard on the minibus: the cafe at the far end is closing for the winter, so pack lunch from October.

Side task: buy spare bootlaces for the kit bag. Two people snapped laces last month and had to tie them with string.

Thing I learned: always write the section number before the observation, not after. Otherwise the notes get muddled when you flip back.
